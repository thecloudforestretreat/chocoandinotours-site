Before you submit

A useful request can be brief. These three points prevent confusion during follow-up.

Do I pay now?

No. We first confirm availability, logistics and the appropriate route before discussing the next booking step.

Can I combine experiences?

Yes. Share your priority first. We can then check whether a nearby second experience fits without creating an unrealistic day.

Are schedules guaranteed?

No schedule is final until hosts, access, transport and current operating conditions have been confirmed for your date.
