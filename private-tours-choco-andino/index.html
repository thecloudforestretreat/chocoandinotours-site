A private Chocó Andino tour is a custom regional plan, not a fixed shared circuit. Tell us your date, starting point, group, main interests and practical needs. We then review what can work together, confirm the people and logistics involved, and provide the operational plan before the visit.

What custom planning changes

Flexibility works best when the important constraints are known from the beginning.

Priority before quantity

We identify the experience that would make the day worthwhile, protect enough time for it, and add another stop only when the geography and schedules support it.

Pace based on the group

Walking, interpretation, meals, rest and photography can be adjusted within the confirmed route. Current trail, road and host conditions still set real limits.

Logistics connected to content

Pickup, route direction, vehicle needs and return time are planned with the visits themselves. Transport is not treated as a separate promise added afterward.

Private does not mean everything is automatically included

Communities, farms, trails and guides have their own schedules and access conditions. A private request gives us the information needed to coordinate a suitable plan; it does not create guaranteed entry, wildlife sightings, unlimited route changes or a universal list of inclusions.

We confirm the route, transport, people involved, meals, entrances, equipment, price and cancellation terms directly for the requested date. Until that confirmation is issued, this page is planning guidance rather than an operational promise.

From request to confirmed route

Three steps keep customization useful and transparent.

1. Share the essentials

Send the date, group size, starting point, main interest, mobility or dietary needs and any required return time. Short, honest details are more useful than a finished itinerary.

2. Review a focused proposal

We compare geography, host or guide availability and transport. If an idea belongs with a Mindo activity or specialist birding team, we make that handoff clear.

3. Confirm the operational version

Review the final route, inclusions, timing, price, payment and cancellation terms. Use that confirmation as the source of truth if a general page differs.

Private-tour questions

These answers help you decide whether custom regional planning is the right fit.

What is a private Chocó Andino tour?

It is a custom route designed around your group's priorities, pace, starting point and confirmed availability. It is not a shared fixed circuit or permission to access every place in the region.

Who is it best for?

Private planning works well for couples, families, small groups, photographers and travelers with specific mobility, language, pacing or subject interests. Share those needs before the route is proposed.

How long should I allow?

A focused day can include one main experience and a compatible addition. A wider regional plan may need more than one day or a base nearer the selected route. The right duration follows geography and purpose.

What must be confirmed?

Confirm the exact route, host and guide availability, transport, pickup and return, access, activity level, meals, equipment, inclusions, price and cancellation terms for your date.
