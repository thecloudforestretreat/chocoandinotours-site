Information that helps us answer clearly

What happens after you contact us

We review the request, identify the appropriate planning path and reply with the next information needed. A first response may ask for a date, pickup point or clearer priority before any route is proposed.

Contacting us does not confirm availability or create a payment obligation. Hosts, transport, access, timing and exact inclusions are confirmed before the plan is treated as final.

Contact questions

These answers explain the first step. Specific routes and availability are confirmed for your date.

Can I contact you in Spanish?

Yes. We can continue the planning conversation in English or Spanish.

How quickly will you answer?

We reply as soon as practical, but response time can vary while the team is guiding, traveling or confirming information with a local host.

Should I use contact or the booking request?

Use contact for a general question. Use the booking request when you already have dates, group size and a clear interest that needs availability review.

Can you arrange transportation?

Transportation can be discussed as part of a route. Share your starting point, pickup needs and required return time so we can assess it realistically.
