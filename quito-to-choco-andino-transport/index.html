Why there is no universal travel-time claim

The UNESCO profile of the Chocó Andino de Pichincha Biosphere Reserve describes a large territory northwest of Quito spanning elevations from 360 to 4,480 meters. That geographic range includes distinct gateways, communities and rural roads. A time estimate becomes useful only after the pickup and exact destination are known, and it remains subject to traffic, weather and current road conditions.
